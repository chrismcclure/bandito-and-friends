# Bandito — Reference Notes

These notes document **permanent identifying characteristics** observed from the real reference photograph at `real/bandito-reference-01.png`. This photograph is the **biological source of truth** for Bandito's appearance.

---

## Physical Observations

### General

- **Young tuxedo kitten** — adolescent, not fully grown; lean and nimble
- **Lean, athletic body** — slender frame with long legs; built for pouncing and climbing
- **Friendly, confident appearance** — open expression; looks ready for adventure, not anxious

### Head and Face

- **Large upright ears** — pointed, black on the outer surface, pink inner fur with fine white tufts
- **Large curious eyes** — round, expressive, greenish-hazel irises with dark pupils; intense and alert
- **Long white whiskers** — arched, fanning wide from the muzzle

### Tuxedo Markings (Critical — Do Not Redesign)

- **White chest and muzzle** — broad white blaze starting at the forehead, narrowing between the eyes, opening into a wide white muzzle
- **Black patches** covering the ears and areas around the eyes
- **Distinctive black marking extending over the bridge of the nose** — begins on the right muzzle (viewer's left), climbs over the nose bridge, and covers the black leathery nose
- **Asymmetrical tuxedo facial markings** — the face is not mirror-symmetric; the nose-bridge marking and chin spot are offset
- **Small black spot on the lower chin** — a distinct "goatee" mark on one side of the lower lip/chin area
- **White paws** — front paws are pure white with visible pink paw pads

### Body Markings

- **White chest** extending to front legs
- **Black "jacket"** beginning at the shoulders and covering the back
- **Tuxedo pattern** is crisp black-and-white contrast — not gray, not blended

### Accessories (Observed in Reference)

- **Black pirate-themed collar** — white skull and crossbones (Jolly Roger) pattern
- **Matching skull-pattern bow tie** on the collar
- **Copper-colored oval ID tag**
- **Two small bells** — one black, one translucent ruby-red

Accessories may appear in reference photos and approved artwork. The **facial markings and tuxedo pattern** are non-negotiable even if accessories are omitted in a specific scene.

### Posture

- **Alert posture** — ears forward, body engaged
- **Naturally heroic stance** — stands tall, chest forward, surveying the environment as though guarding something important
- Does not look like a passive house cat — looks like a protector scanning for threats

---

## Personality

Bandito is:

- **Optimistic** — believes the best outcome is always achievable
- **Brave** — faces perceived danger without hesitation
- **Friendly** — warm toward his team and his city
- **Dramatic** — narrates situations with gravity and flair
- **Always believes he can protect Meow City** — his mission is sincere, not performative
- **Inspires the rest of the team** — others follow his confidence

Personality is expressed through **pose and context**, not exaggerated cartoon faces. Bandito is serious when the moment calls for it — even when the moment is absurd.
