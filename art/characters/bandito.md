# Character Overview

Bandito is the optimistic leader of the Meow City protection squad. He sincerely believes he and his team defend a vast metropolis from ancient evils and everyday threats. To the audience, he is a house cat. To Bandito, he is a hero.

He narrates situations dramatically, inspires his teammates, and never doubts that Meow City can be saved. Comedy comes from his complete sincerity — never from Bandito breaking character.

---

# Physical Appearance

Bandito is a **young tuxedo kitten** with a **lean, athletic body**. He has **large upright ears**, **large curious eyes**, a **white chest**, and **white paws**. His black-and-white coat has **crisp contrast** — not gray, not softened.

He appears friendly and confident. His default physical presence is **alert** — ready to act, not lounging passively.

---

# Facial Markings

Facial markings are **the most important visual identifier**. They must remain consistent in every piece of artwork.

## Permanent Features

- **White blaze** — wide white area starting at the forehead, narrowing between the eyes, opening into a broad white muzzle
- **Black patches** around the eyes and covering the outer ears
- **Distinctive black marking over the bridge of the nose** — extends from the muzzle up over the nose bridge and covers the nose itself; this marking is **asymmetrical**; **must always be preserved; never omit or simplify away**
- **Small black chin spot** — offset "goatee" mark on one side of the lower chin/lip
- **Asymmetrical tuxedo facial markings overall** — do not mirror or simplify into a symmetric tuxedo face

These facial markings are **non-negotiable** in every sprite, reference sheet, and production asset.

## Eyes

- Large, round, expressive
- Greenish-hazel tone
- Dark pupils
- Convey curiosity, confidence, and determination

## Ears

- Large for his head size — reads as young and alert
- Black outer fur, pink inner fur
- Upright and forward when engaged

---

# Body Shape

- **Lean and athletic** — long legs, slender torso, built for climbing and pouncing
- **Young kitten proportions** — not stocky, not fully grown
- **White chest** flowing into white front legs
- **White neck-and-shoulder band** — a white marking that wraps around the back of his neck and shoulders, extending just behind where a collar would sit; this is part of his **canonical tuxedo pattern** and must **never be omitted**
- **Black back and shoulders** — the "jacket" of the tuxedo pattern, framing the white neck band
- **White paws** with visible pad detail when applicable
- **Long black tail** — solid black, held with purpose

Silhouette should be immediately recognizable: upright ears, white chest wedge, white neck band visible from front and three-quarter views, compact heroic stance.

---

# Personality

- **Optimistic** — expects victory
- **Brave** — leads from the front
- **Friendly** — loyal to his team
- **Dramatic** — treats briefings, patrols, and battles with anime gravity
- **Believes in the mission** — protecting Meow City is his purpose
- **Inspirational** — the team trusts him because he never wavers

Bandito does not know he is funny. He is never sarcastic. He is never cowardly.

---

# Signature Expressions

Expressions are **sincere and readable**, not exaggerated cartoon takes.

| Expression | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| **Determined** | Eyes wide, ears forward, chin slightly raised — default hero look |
| **Dramatic narration** | Mouth open mid-speech, one paw lifted, addressing the team |
| **Alert** | Ears swiveled, body low and ready — threat detected |
| **Triumphant** | Chest out, eyes bright — Meow City is safe again |
| **Reverent** | Soft eyes, still posture — witnessing Tortellini's "strategy" |

Avoid: winking, tongue-out silliness, horror faces, or breaking the fourth wall.

---

# Signature Hero Poses

| Pose | Use |
|------|-----|
| **Leader briefing** | Standing elevated, chest out, addressing the team |
| **Protector stance** | In front of teammates, facing a perceived threat |
| **Surveying Meow City** | Looking upward or across the room like a general scanning the horizon |
| **Victory freeze** | Center frame, paw raised or on hip, team assembled behind him |
| **Pounce ready** | Rear low, front paws forward — battle engaged |

Poses should feel like anime or NES hero staging — deliberate, weighted, cinematic.

---

# Animation Notes

- Bandito moves with **confidence** — snappy entrances, held poses, clean silhouettes
- **Facial markings stay fixed** during animation — the nose-bridge mark does not slide or disappear between frames; the white neck-and-shoulder band remains visible from applicable angles
- **Ears and tail** carry emotion — forward for focus, up for alert, still for dramatic beats
- Scale him at **cat height** in environments — Meow City furniture is monumental
- When paired with the Threat Board or team, Bandito is usually **center or elevated**
- Refer to `art/style-guide.md` for global animation feel and **Pixel Art Style** requirements

# Image Generation Rules

When generating or commissioning artwork of Bandito:

1. **Use pixel art** — see `art/style-guide.md` → **Pixel Art Style**; reject painted or vector-style output
2. **Preserve facial markings exactly** — especially the **black marking over the bridge of the nose** (never omit) and the asymmetrical tuxedo pattern
3. **Preserve the white neck-and-shoulder band** — wraps around the back of the neck and shoulders, extending just behind the collar; canonical tuxedo marking, never omitted
4. **Do not simplify or redesign the tuxedo pattern** — no symmetric faces, no generic black-and-white cat
5. **Match the real reference photograph** at `art/references/bandito/real/bandito-reference-01.png`
6. **Match approved official pixel-art references** in `art/references/bandito/official/` — especially `bandito-sprite-sheet.png`
7. **Preserve proportions** — young, lean, large ears; not chubby, not adult-sized
8. **Preserve eye color and ear shape** from references
9. **Personality through pose**, not distorted expressions
10. Read `art/style-guide.md` and `art/references/bandito/notes.md` before every generation
11. **Never redesign** unless explicitly instructed by the project owner

Future artwork should resemble **both** the real reference photographs **and** the approved official pixel-art reference once one exists. If stylization differs from the photo, the official pixel-art reference governs — but facial markings, the nose-bridge mark, the white neck-and-shoulder band, and tuxedo asymmetry must survive stylization intact.
