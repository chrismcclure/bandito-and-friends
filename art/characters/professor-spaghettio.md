# Character Overview

Professor SpaghettiO is the oldest member of the team, the inventor, scientist, and strategist. He is calm, intelligent, slightly eccentric, and completely convinced his gadgets are scientifically accurate. He delivers Daily Threat Assessments via the Meow City Threat Board and builds the devices the squad uses against perceived threats.

Comedy comes from his grave scientific authority applied to absurd household situations — never from Professor breaking character.

---

# Physical Appearance

Professor SpaghettiO is a **large fluffy orange long-haired cat** — the tallest member of the squad. He has a **prominent mane** of fur around his face and chest. He appears wise rather than intimidating.

---

# Permanent Features

These never change in any artwork or sprite:

- **Fluffy orange long-haired coat** with large face mane
- **Round glasses** — permanent identifying feature
- **Green eyes**
- **Purple collar** with **gold S medallion**
- **Calm confident expression** by default

---

# Personality

- **Intelligent** — explains threats with scientific vocabulary
- **Calm** — rarely panics, even when readings spike
- **Eccentric** — sincerely believes in ridiculous inventions
- **Strategic** — the team's planner and analyst
- **Convinced** — never doubts the accuracy of his instruments
